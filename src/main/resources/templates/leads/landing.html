Questions

Can Zamu answer our phone calls?

Yes. We connect Zamu to your existing business number through your phone provider, so customers call the number they already know. Zamu answers in English or Swahili, books, takes M-Pesa deposits, and transfers the call to your staff when a person is needed. Voice is in early access on the Growth, Business and Enterprise plans.

Are calls recorded?

No. Zamu keeps written notes of each call: who called, what they asked and what was done. Callers are told at the start that they're speaking to an automated assistant and that notes are taken.

Will my customers know they're talking to an assistant?

Yes. On WhatsApp the first message says it's an automated assistant and tells them how to reach a person: they can type "person" or "mtu" at any time. On a call, Zamu says so when it answers, and the caller can ask for a person at any time.

Can I keep my current WhatsApp number?

Usually, yes. Zamu runs on your business number through Meta's official WhatsApp platform. On the kickoff call we check whether your staff can keep using the WhatsApp Business app on the same number.

What happens when Zamu doesn't know the answer?

It says it isn't sure and connects the customer to your team. Your staff see the whole chat in the Zamu dashboard and reply from your own number. Out of hours, it takes a message and tells the customer when you'll reply.

Where does the money go?

Straight to your own M-Pesa till or paybill. We never receive or hold your customers' payments.

How long does setup take?

Five working days from the kickoff call. Meta and Safaricom approvals can take longer; if they do, we go live with answers and handover first and switch on bookings and payments when the approvals arrive.

Can my staff use it on their phones?

Yes. The dashboard works in any phone browser, so receptionists can take over chats wherever they are.

Google Calendar

Zamu checks the calendar before offering a time and again just before booking it, so appointments staff add by hand are never double-booked. Bookings appear in the calendar, marked held until confirmed.

Google sign-in isn't set up on this Zamu installation. Set ZAMU_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID and ZAMU_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET, and register this redirect URI on the Google OAuth client: https://…/admin/calendar/callback. Until then, slots use opening hours and Zamu's own bookings only.
